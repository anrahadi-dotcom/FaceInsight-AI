      FAQ

      Questions, answered

      No. Your image is read straight into the browser tab, turned into pixels, and thrown away when you close it. There is no upload endpoint and no storage — the only network requests are for the AI model files themselves.

      The scanner runs real facial-landmark detection (Google's MediaPipe) to measure symmetry, golden-ratio proportions, canthal tilt and skin texture. Those are geometric estimates tuned by eye against a handful of test photos — fun and directional, not a medical or scientific measurement of attractiveness.

      Front-facing, sharp, evenly lit, and with your whole head inside the frame. Avoid heavy filters, extreme angles and harsh side shadows — they all distort landmark positions and make the score wobble. If the photo is soft or low-resolution the scanner will tell you.

      It doesn't — gender is auto-detected from the photo (face-api.js) purely to pick the right tier labels and tip wording. It changes the copy, not the underlying scores, and it defaults to male if detection fails.

      No install, no account, no API key. The first scan downloads the MediaPipe and face-api.js model files from a public CDN, so you need an internet connection on first load — after that the browser caches them.
